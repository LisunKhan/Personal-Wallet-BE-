# Personal Digital Wallet

**A secure personal digital wallet where users can store passwords, documents, personal notes, credentials, and secrets — all encrypted and accessible only by them.**

## 💎 Why This Is a Great Idea

Everyone has multiple accounts, and it's easy to forget passwords. Personal documents like NID, passports, and certificates are often scattered across different devices and platforms. Important notes and secrets are randomly saved in messaging apps, note-taking apps, or physical notepads, without proper encryption. This project solves a global problem by providing a single, secure place for everything.

## 🧩 Core Features (MVP)

### 1. Password Manager
*   Save website logins and passwords
*   Autofill browser extension
*   Category-wise password storage
*   Strong-password generator
*   Duplicate password warning

### 2. Secure Document Storage
*   Store your important documents such as NID, Passport, Certificates, Bank statements, Tax files, Agreements, and Personal photos.
*   Encrypt everything to ensure your documents are safe and secure.

### 3. Personal Notes
*   A safe place for your secret notes, OTP backups, recovery codes, ideas, and bank details.
*   Everything is end-to-end encrypted.

### 4. Two-Level Security
*   Master password
*   Biometric login
*   2FA (OTP, authenticator)
*   Device-based verification

### 5. Emergency Access
*   In case of an emergency, you can nominate one trusted person to access your wallet.
*   Access is granted only after a time delay (e.g., 7 days).

### 6. Device Sync
*   Your wallet syncs across all your devices, including mobile, web, and desktop.
